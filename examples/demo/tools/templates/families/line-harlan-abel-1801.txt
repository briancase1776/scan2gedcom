NUMBERING AND PARENTAGE
================================================================
Ada numbers each generation from Abel (1). An entry belongs to the
nearest entry above it with the next lower number: Josiah and Clara
(3) to Ezra (2), Walter (4) to Josiah, Howard (5) to Walter, Carol
and Daniel (6) to Howard. The numbers on PDF 5 run without a break.

Josiah's mother [I]. Ada gives Ezra two wives and says "By Sarah he
had Clara", but not who Josiah's mother was. Ezra is his father by
the numbering; Martha is worked out: Josiah was born in 1850, after
Ezra's first marriage (1848) and before Martha's death (1856) and his
second marriage (1858). In the data he is "inferred" in the family of
Ezra and Martha.

Silas Pike has no number: he is Sarah's son by Amos Pike, not Abel's
descendant, and was "raised by Ezra" (PDF 5). In the data he is a
child of Amos and Sarah, and also in Ezra and Sarah's family under
"step", with Ezra (the husband) as his step-parent; never Ezra's own
child.

Daniel: "b. 1946, adopted" (PDF 5), under Howard. Ada names no birth
parents for him, and he is not a step-child: Howard and Grace adopted
him. In the data he is "adopted" in their family. Carol's entry has
no such word.
