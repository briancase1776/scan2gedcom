DEED - Linden Co. Deed Book B, p. 40

This Indenture made the 2nd day of March 1830 between Abel
Harlan and Ruth his wife of Linden County of the one part and
Ezra Harlan of the same of the other part, for $200, 80 acres
on Pell Creek.
Abel Harlan (seal)
Ruth (her X mark) Harlan (seal)
Witness: Jno. Pell
