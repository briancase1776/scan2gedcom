DESCENDANTS OF ABEL HARLAN

2. Ezra Harlan (see page 1) m. (1) 1848 Martha Quill, who d. 1856;
m. (2) 1858 Sarah Dunn, b. 1834, dau. of Jacob and Hannah Dunn of
Linden Co., and widow of Amos Pike. Her son Silas Pike, b. 1855,
was raised by Ezra. By Sarah he had Clara.
3. Josiah Harlan, b. 1850, d. 1921, m. 1874 Emma Rusk, b. Sept. 17
4. Walter Harlan, b. 12 Jan 1878, d. 1950, m. 1905 Ida Venn
5. Howard Harlan, b. 1912, m. 1938 Grace Lomax, b. 1918
6. Carol Harlan, b. 1941
6. Daniel Harlan, b. 1946, adopted
3. Clara Harlan, b. 1860, d. 1862

2. Lydia Harlan (see page 1) m. ___ Stewart. Nothing more is known
of her.
