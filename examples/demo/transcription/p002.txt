ABEL HARLAN - 1801

Abel Harlan was born 4 Mar 1801 in Linden Co. His father is not
known for certain but family tradition say he was a son of Thomas
Harlan. Abel married Ruth Pell on 12 June 1824 at her fathers
house on Pell Creek.

1. Abel Harlan, b. 4 Mar 1801, d. 9 Oct 1869, m. Ruth Pell
2. Ezra Harlan, b. 2 May 1825
2. Lydia Harlan, b. ___ 1827, m. ___ Stewart
2. Thomas Harlan, b. 1830, d. young

Abel and Ruth sold there land on Pell Creek to Ezra in 1830. He
was a farmer and a "great hand with horses." Ruth died 1 Feb 1871
and is buried beside him in the Pell Creek burying ground, but
some say she was buried at Mt. Zion.
