    WALTER HARLAN

    Walter Harlan, 72, died Sunday at his home on Pell Creek. He
    farmed there all his life. He and the former Ida Venn were
    married in 1906. Survivors are his wife; a son, Howard, of
    Fenwick; and two grandchildren. Burial was in the Pell Creek
    burying ground.
    [handwritten: Mar. 1950]
