context [I], say which parent and why under NUMBERING AND PARENTAGE,
and list the child in that family's "inferred". A parent the book
only guesses at ("family tradition says") is linked the same way,
with the child in "inferred".

LIVING: <the LIVING rule from DATA-FORMAT.txt, with the project's
