FILES
  One file per PDF page: p001.txt, p002.txt, ... (three digits; four
  if the book has 1000 pages or more).
  Files are named by PDF page, not by the book's own page number,
  because the book's numbering drifts from the PDF's and hand-numbered
  pages repeat. Cite pages by PDF page.

HEADER
  Each file starts with a header, then a line of 64 dashes, then the
  page:

    PDF page:   2
    Book page:  1          (the page number as shown, e.g. 8A for an
                            inserted page; "none" if absent. If a page
                            has both a typed and a handwritten number
                            and they differ, give both, handwritten
                            first: "128 (handwritten); 130 (typed)".)
    Kind:       typed narrative
    Notes:      (anything about the page itself; "none" if nothing)
    ----------------------------------------------------------------

  Kind is one of: cover, typed narrative, typed record (the author's
  typed copy of a record), record copy (photocopy of a record, form or
  index card), newspaper clipping, handwritten, photo, map, chart,
  blank. A page can have more than one, separated by " + ". The
  author's own numbered descendant lists count as typed narrative.

  Problems with the scan itself (a missing page, a page scanned twice,
  pages out of order) go in Notes, and in SCAN-NOTES.txt.

BODY
  - Word for word, line for line, as on the page.
  - Spelling, punctuation, capitals and typing errors are kept exactly
    as typed. Nothing is corrected here; corrections belong in the
    group sheets.
  - Spacing is not kept: runs of spaces are collapsed to one, and
    paragraph indents are dropped. Blank lines between paragraphs are
    kept.
  - Underlined text is marked _like this_.
  - Blanks typed for an unknown name or date are kept as underscores,
    e.g. "m. ___ Stewart". (Underscores with no closing one are a
    blank; _word_ is underlining.)
  - Ditto marks are typed as ".
  - Bold, a change of typeface, and highlighting are described in
    Notes, not marked in the body.
  - Names in handwriting are spelled exactly as written, even when the
    typed pages spell them differently.
  - Words hyphenated at a line end stay hyphenated, as on the page.
  - Fractions as on the page: ½ stays ½.
  - The page number at the top or bottom of the page goes in the
    header only.
  - Columns (lists, tables, estate sales) are separated with " | ".

EDITORIAL MARKS (always in square brackets)
  [illegible]            a word that cannot be read
  [illegible: 3 words]   several words that cannot be read
  word[?]                a reading that is uncertain
  [photo: ...]           a photograph, with its caption or a description
  [map: ...]             a map, described the same way; other pictures
                         likewise, e.g. [advertisement: ...]
  [handwritten: ...]     handwriting added to a typed page
  [struck: ...]          text that is struck out but still readable
  [margin: ...]          a note in the margin
  [blank]                a page or space left blank
  If the author typed square brackets, the Notes line says so.

PRIVACY
  The book may name living people. The transcription stays in a
  private repository until living-people status has been checked.
